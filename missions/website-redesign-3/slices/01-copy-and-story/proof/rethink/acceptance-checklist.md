## Observed checks and limits

Earlier `public-edges-before.json`16PASS14FAIL includes two harness false positives on mobile-hidden controls and is retained, not silently replaced.

Screenshot readers cannot validate motion timing, security review, inbox accuracy, real billing or conversion. They are model sessions, not human participants. Local proof checks do not prove installer safety or production deployment. Scheduler inspection establishes code conditions, not that any owner's installed agent works. The command is inspected/copied only.

Production nginx/Docker/build replacement and removal of old production assets belong to later approved implementation. No synthetic substitute build is labelled production acceptance. Final fixed-copy checks must use the final commit and cannot inherit the frozen reader outcome as a new pass.
