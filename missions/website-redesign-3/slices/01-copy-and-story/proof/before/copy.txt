H2: What it needs, sends and costs
H3: Your Mac
P: Apple Silicon with macOS 26 Tahoe or later.
P: The installer stops on anything else.
H3: Gmail
P: You sign in with Google in your browser. zero never sees your password.
P: Google may warn that zero is unverified. It hasn’t finished Google’s review.
H3: Your mail
P: zero runs on your Mac. To sort a thread, it sends Jev the sender, subject, a short preview, your rules and what it has learned from you.
P: The zero project runs no server that receives your email. Privacy policy.
