Set up zero.

You’ll need a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later, a Gmail account, and a TypeSafe Jev API key.

Jev sorts your mail. The app is free. Jev usage is charged to your TypeSafe account.

Sorting sends email text to Jev. Optional reply drafts send it to your chosen AI provider. How your data is used

  1. Install from Terminal.

    zero isn’t Apple-notarized. The installer may add Homebrew and command-line tools. Google may show an unverified-app warning when you connect Gmail. Read the script first.

    Open Terminal on your Mac and run:

    curl -fsSL https://zero.headless.com/install | bash

  2. Connect Gmail and add your key.

    Open zero from the menu bar and choose Connect your first inbox. Add your Jev key in Settings → Sorting engine.

  3. Try your first sweep.

    Choose Run zero now. Review what it kept and archived before setting a daily schedule.

Before you run the installer

zero is ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized. It isn’t in the App Store. Google sign-in is included, but Google may show an unverified-app warning. Only continue if you’re comfortable granting mailbox access.

The installer may add Homebrew, Python, Node, the Google Workspace CLI and an agent CLI. Current onboarding checks for Claude Code even if you don’t use drafts.

Prefer a manual download? See GitHub Releases and the full setup notes.

A few things to know.

Does my email stay on my Mac?

No. zero runs on your Mac, but sends thread text to TypeSafe’s Jev for sorting. If you ask for a reply draft, that content goes to your chosen AI provider. The zero project doesn’t run a server that receives your email. Read the privacy policy and your providers’ terms.

What if it archives something important?

The AI can make mistakes. Archived mail stays searchable in Gmail’s All Mail, and each run has a dated recovery label. Use zero’s Undo view to restore a run. Review early results and adjust Settings → Rules to suit your inbox.

Can zero send email for me?

Only when you ask it to. Reply drafts are optional and use a separate AI provider. You review and edit the draft, then click Send reply. zero doesn’t send replies on its own.

Can I use more than one Gmail account?

Yes. Connect your accounts in zero and see their conversations together in the Open loops panel. Click a conversation to open it in Gmail.
