Privacy

Last updated 21 September 2026

The short version

  • zero runs on your Mac, using your own Google account and the services configured in its settings.
  • There is no zero server, no zero account, and no analytics. The project does not operate a server that receives your email.
  • Your mail is read on your Mac. To sort it, relevant thread text is sent to TypeSafe's Jev service using the key configured in Settings → Sorting engine, subject to TypeSafe's terms.
  • zero only ever asks Google for Gmail access and your basic identity. Nothing else.
  • zero does not sell your data or use it for its own advertising or model training. Each external provider's handling is governed by that provider's terms.

zero is a macOS menu-bar app that checks your connected inboxes on the schedule you choose, keeps conversations that still need action, and archives the rest — reversibly, never deleting. This page explains exactly what it touches and where that data goes. The app runs on your computer, but sorting and optional drafting can involve external services you configure.

Who runs zero

zero is made by Tayo Onabule (Drewl). It is an open-source app you install and run on your own Mac, not a hosted service. When we say "we," we mean the people who write the code — not an operator sitting between you and your mail, because there isn't one.

What zero connects to

When you add an account, zero signs you in through Google's standard OAuth screen and requests only these permissions:

  • Gmail (read & modify) — gmail.modify. This lets zero read your threads to judge them, archive a thread (by removing its Inbox label), apply your category labels, and — only when you ask — create and send a reply draft. It is the single scope that covers everything zero does with mail. zero never permanently deletes a message; archiving is a label change you can undo with one Gmail search.
  • Your identity — openid, userinfo.email, and userinfo.profile. Used to know which account you signed in as and to show your name and profile photo in the app. Nothing more.

zero does not request access to Google Drive, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Tasks, or your contacts. If you ever see those on the consent screen, something is wrong — tell us.

Where your data goes

Your email data travels between your Mac and the services needed for the features you use:

  • To and from Google — zero reads your mailbox through Google's Gmail API, directly from your Mac, using your sign-in. This is your own data moving between you and Google.
  • To external AI services — keep-or-archive decisions always go to TypeSafe's Jev under the API key configured in Settings → Sorting engine. If you request a reply draft, zero sends the relevant thread text to the provider configured in Settings → AI engine, through the agent CLI you have signed into (Claude Code, Codex, or opencode). Those providers handle the text under their own terms and policies.

These requests are made from your Mac. The data does not pass through any server we operate, because we don't operate one.

What zero stores, and where

  • Sign-in tokens live only on your Mac — in your login keychain or in an encrypted file in zero's application-support folder. They never leave your machine except to talk to Google.
  • Your settings, labels, and the day's sorting results are kept in local files on your Mac so the app can show you what it did.
  • We do not operate a database that stores your information. The project cannot delete data held by Google or by an AI provider.

Limited Use

zero's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. zero uses Gmail data to provide the in-app features you request — sorting your inbox and drafting replies — and does not use it for advertising or sell it. The relevant thread text is transferred to TypeSafe's Jev for sorting and, when you request a draft, to the provider configured in Settings → AI engine.

What we never do

  • We don't sell your data, or share it for advertising.
  • We don't read your mail. No human at zero can — we never receive it.
  • We don't use your mail to train AI models. Whether an external provider retains or uses content is governed by that provider's terms and settings.
  • We don't track you. No analytics, no telemetry, no usage pings.

Your control

  • Revoke access at any time from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions — find zero in the list and remove it. zero loses access immediately.
  • Remove an account inside zero, or quit and delete the app. Deleting zero's application-support folder removes every local trace, including the stored tokens.
  • Because we hold nothing, there's no account to close on our side and nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

Children

zero isn't directed at anyone under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live), and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and note it in the app's release notes. zero is open source, so every change is also visible in its public history.
